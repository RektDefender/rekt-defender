HOW THE LEADERBOARD WORKS

Step 01
PAY & PLAY
You pay 0.10 USDC via x402 / OpenFacilitator. This creates a unique verified session ID tied to your wallet address and the payment transaction. No payment = no session = no score saved.
Step 02
SCORE VERIFIED
When your game ends, your score is submitted with your session ID. Our server checks: Is the session ID real? Has it been used before? Does it match your wallet? If all three pass — score is written to the leaderboard. If any fail — score is rejected.
Step 03
ONLY YOUR BEST COUNTS
Play as many games as you like — each costs 0.10 USDC. Only your best single game score that week appears on the leaderboard. You can improve your position by playing again and beating your own score.
Step 04
MONDAY PAYOUT
Every Monday at 00:00 UTC the leaderboard locks. Top 3 wallets are identified. REKT tokens (bought from the prize pool using that week's fees) are sent directly to the winning wallet addresses within 24 hours. No claiming required — it just arrives.
Step 05
HALL OF FAME FOREVER
Winners are moved to the Hall of Fame permanently with their wallet address, score, prize amount, and the on-chain transaction proving payment was made. The weekly leaderboard resets. The Hall of Fame never does.
FAQ

Can I play multiple times? Yes. Each game costs 0.10 USDC. Only your best score counts for the week.

What if two players tie? The score submitted earlier wins the higher position.

When exactly does the week reset? Monday 00:00 UTC sharp. Scores submitted after that count for the new week.

How do I know prizes were paid? Every payout is recorded in the Hall of Fame with the on-chain transaction ID. Verify it yourself on Solana Explorer.

What if I win but give a wrong wallet? We pay the wallet that played the game. Connect the right wallet before you play.
