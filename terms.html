Section 02

WHAT REKT DEFENDER IS

Rekt Defender is a skill-based browser game where players compete for the highest score. Players have the option to pay a game fee in USDC to participate in the competitive weekly leaderboard. The top 3 players by score each week receive REKT tokens as prizes.

A free demo mode is also available with no payment required. Demo mode scores do not appear on the live leaderboard and are not eligible for prizes.

Rekt Defender is a game of skill. Scores are determined entirely by player ability and gameplay performance. There is no element of chance or randomness that determines competitive outcomes. Prize eligibility is based solely on skill-based score rankings.

Section 04

PAYMENTS & GAME FEES

Each paid game requires a fee of 0.10 USDC payable via your connected Solana wallet. Payments are processed via x402 / OpenFacilitator on the Solana blockchain.

Fee Allocation

Each 0.10 USDC game fee is manually processed and split as follows:

  • 50% — Used to purchase REKT tokens on the open market for the weekly prize pool
  • 20% — Used to purchase and permanently burn REKT tokens
  • 30% — Retained by the operator for development, hosting, and operational costs

No Refunds

All game fees are non-refundable. Once a payment is confirmed on the Solana blockchain, it cannot be reversed. By approving a payment in your wallet, you acknowledge that you are paying for the right to participate in a single game session and that this fee is non-refundable regardless of game outcome.

Failed Sessions

If a technical error prevents your game session from starting after payment is confirmed, please contact us via X DM with your wallet address and transaction ID. We will review and issue a credit for a future game session at our discretion. We do not guarantee refunds in any circumstances.

💡 Note on Blockchain Transactions
Payments are processed on the Solana blockchain. While Solana fees are minimal, you are responsible for any network fees associated with your transactions. We have no control over blockchain network performance or transaction processing times.
Section 05

PRIZES & LEADERBOARD

The top 3 players by score each week are eligible to receive REKT token prizes. The prize pool consists of REKT tokens purchased from the open market using 50% of that week's total game fees.

Prize Split

  • 1st place — 60% of the weekly prize pool
  • 2nd place — 30% of the weekly prize pool
  • 3rd place — 10% of the weekly prize pool

Prize Distribution

Prizes are distributed to the winning wallet addresses within 24 hours of each Monday 00:00 UTC reset. Prizes are sent directly to the wallet address connected during gameplay. We are not responsible for prizes sent to incorrect wallets due to player error.

Score Verification

All scores are verified against a unique paid session ID before being recorded on the leaderboard. Scores submitted without a valid, unused session ID matching the submitting wallet address will be rejected. We reserve the right to disqualify any score we determine to be fraudulent, manipulated, or submitted in bad faith.

Prize Value

Prizes are denominated in REKT tokens. The USD value of prizes fluctuates with the market price of REKT and is not guaranteed. We make no representation about the present or future value of REKT tokens. Prize amounts shown on the Site are estimates based on current token price and actual amounts received may differ.

Leaderboard Disputes

Our determination of leaderboard rankings and prize winners is final. In the event of a dispute, contact hello@rektdefender.lol within 48 hours of the weekly reset. We will review disputes at our sole discretion.

✓ Hall of Fame
All weekly winners are recorded permanently in the public Hall of Fame at rektdefender.lol/leaderboard. This record includes wallet addresses, scores, and on-chain transaction proof of prize payment.
